ABOUT FERTEMPEH

Fertempeh is a small business that sells fermented tempeh as said before. Aside from being a school project, it is also a part-time job (not real guys this is only for a project). Here we sell various kinds of fermented tempeh. Since tempes are known for being rich in fiber and are gut friendly, our purpose is to sell absolutely tasty yet healthy foods, keeping things balanced. This business stand was originally made for a school assignment, but eventually was made into a real business (no its not). Fertempeh could be included as a micro business in terms of UMKM (Usaha Mikro Kecil Menengah). We are also available for events and we do collabs with other creators. Currently, we have 6 different kinds of tempeh available for sale, consists of tempe kedelai hitam, tempe kacang edamame, tempe kacang hijau, tempe kacang tanah and tempe gembus, find out more about it below!

Tempe Kacang Edamame

This tempeh is made with kacang edamame as the raw material. It's richer in protein and calories than your average tempeh! Some would say it's even better than tempe with soy beans (kacang kedelai).

1 pak = Rp 10.000

Tempe Kedelai Hitam

This tempeh is made with kacang kedelai hitam as the raw material. It's got an interesting look and taste - a bitter-ish flavor as an after taste. Give it a try...

1 pak = Rp 10.000

Tempe Kacang Hijau

This tempeh is made with kacang hijau as the raw material. It's got an interesting look.

1 pak = Rp 10.000

Tempe Kacang Tanah

This tempeh is made with kacang tanah as the raw material. Kacang tanah is fancied by a lot of locals for it's salty taste. You might even like it!

1 pak = Rp 10.000

Tempe Kacang Merah

This tempeh is made with kacang merah as the raw material. Like it's name, tempe kacang merah has the color red all over, which makes them seem odd compared to the other tempe(s).

1 pak = Rp 10.000

Tempe Gembus

A new addition to Fertempeh, this one is a tad bit different. The raw material used for this tempe is tofu pulp. Interesting, no? This is the most different out of the other 5. Interested yet?

1 pak = Rp 10.000
